Timing results for inserting random strings of length 5.

N = 5000:
  ULLMap: 0.07 sec
  BSTMap: 0.00 sec
  Java's Built-in TreeMap: 0.00 sec
  Java's Built-in HashMap: 0.00 sec

N = 100000:
  ULLMap: Stack Overflow -- couldn't add 100000 strings of length 5.
  BSTMap: 0.03 sec
  Java's Built-in TreeMap: 0.04 sec
  Java's Built-in HashMap: 0.02 sec

Observations:
- ULLMap is an unsorted linked list with O(N) insertion and its recursive
  Entry.get overflows the call stack around 100k random insertions.
- BSTMap, TreeMap, and HashMap all handle 100k insertions easily.
- For random insertion order, a simple BST has O(log N) expected height,
  so BSTMap is comparable to the built-in TreeMap.
